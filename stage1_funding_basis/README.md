# QT Stage1: one-week risk warehouse — can funding rent survive hedge basis?

This is a NEW economic class relative to the earlier liquidation, fee/keeper and atomic routes: lending balance-sheet risk-bearing capacity to perpetual longs via short-perp exposure earns *funding*. Capital stays at risk over time. Flash loans cannot finance the required holding period.

Prior PR106 sampled complete real Hyperliquid BTC/ETH/SOL funding in **September 10–17 training** and **October 10–17 independent holdout 2026** (168 hourly payments each). Holdout cumulative positive funding to an unhedged short, as percent of notional: BTC 0.11781695%, ETH 0.09624362%, SOL 0.11795222%. These are NOT trading profits. A Markov positive-funding predictor improved holdout Brier scores relative to an unconditional baseline, but it did NOT assess the cost of constructing a short-perp/long-spot hedge.

**This experiment:** freeze same holdout Oct 10 00:00 UTC through Oct 17 00:00, **not selected for attractive price movements**. Collect original public Hyperliquid hourly perp closing prices and funding settlement history and Coinbase public BTC-USD, ETH-USD, SOL-USD hourly spot closes. One base-unit portfolio: long q=1 spot, short q=1 HL perp at start, exit together at end. Compute change in spot value plus profit/loss of short perp plus a funded-perp payment series approximated by rate multiplied by its hourly observed perp closing price. Divide by initial USD spot notional for bps sensitivity. Compare gross at hypothetical combined all-leg roundtrip fee budgets of 0, 5, 10, 20 and 40 bps of initial notional.

**Strong baseline:** buy spot at executable ask and short perp at executable bid, liquidate at inverse sides, using time-consistent USD vs USDC FX and actual fees, friction, borrow, collateral/margin, liquidation probabilities and custody. Hourly close data alone cannot accomplish any of that. A *positive* close proxy is merely a necessary hypothesis; a negative zero-fee proxy at this fixed horizon disqualifies the hold-long strategy for this hindsight window.

Public sources: https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint (fundingHistory and candleSnapshot), Coinbase Exchange historic candles REST. **Data limitations:** HL candle close is NOT funding mark or last trade executable fill; Coinbase prices USD, HL USDC; exact hourly path can have gaps even with HTTP200; early/late entry changes basis; spot purchase and separate perp margin are NOT flash-loan financed. Historical risk management must prevent liquidation under high volatility. Valuation includes counterparty venue risk, margin and liquidation rights.

**Protocol restrictions:** 9 public read-only HTTP requests maximum; no API key, no Blockmachine RU, wallet, orders, live trading, external outreach or new money. Archive request and response SHA. Offline regression before one PR-open empirical run, all later synchronization offline-only. QT Stage1 remains open and no Stage2 promotion.
