# Stage 1 independent mechanism: Polymarket binary complete-set cash screen

Outcome-invariant cash is theoretically possible if a YES and a NO claim **in the same market condition** can be bought for less than the $1 collateral paid by either mutually exclusive exhaustive outcome, after taker fees, buying costs, onchain redemption and regulatory access. This is independent of liquidation, ERC-4626 share valuation, wind-down issuer redemption and Beefy harvest service rewards.

**Empirical public tests**: Freeze the highest-volume 10 and highest-liquidity 10 among the publicly returned top-100 per ranking of active, accepting-order, standard non-negRisk YES/NO with at least $1000 liquidity and volume; no selection by book inefficiency. Request actual public CLOB /book for both tokens, validate exact condition ID and asset IDs. Take *minimum ask over all valid positive-size levels*, consume actual asks until [10,50,100,500] paired shares are filled, calculate cash paid **separately on each side**. Charge upper category worst-case taker fee at **7% per share * price * (1-price)** (Polymarket July 2026 fees), rounding upward to 1e-5 USDC, plus **$1 screening-only estimated Polygon redemption/gas reserve**. This is intentionally not a verified gas cost and does NOT remove maker competition.

Caution: independent sequential HTTP snapshots and two live buy orders are **not atomic**, so even conservative positive screenshots are *not executable risk-free profits*. Mint/merge/redemption rights, settlement disputes, actual taker fees, currency custody, geo permissions and fragmentation must be empirically validated; there is no claim of live legal access. CLOB may serve stale/depleted books. Report timestamp skew and hashes when provided. The scan covers at most 20 selected markets, not the full venue, and **does not measure recurrence** without repeated historical snapshots.

Sources: https://docs.polymarket.com/faq (fully collateralized YES+NO), https://institute.polymarket.com/data (Gamma to CLOB token ID pipeline), https://help.polymarket.com/en/articles/13364478-trading-fees (July 10 2026 category fee schedule).

HTTP GET only. No accounts, signup, wallets, signing, placing or cancelling orders, payments, bypass or trades. Freeze metrics and write all original raw requests/responses and hashes. Do not bypass 401/403/429; mark unavailable rather than negative.
